# Reply template pilot

## Results

Measurement window: April 1–30, 2026. A reopening is a ticket that returns to
the open state within seven days of its first closure.

| Queue | Closed tickets | Reopened within seven days |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Template pilot | 90 | 9 |
| Comparison | 120 | 18 |

## Method

The pilot used a queue with a higher share of standard billing requests. The
comparison queue handled a wider range of issues. Tickets were assigned by
existing routing rules. The evaluation did not randomly assign tickets.
