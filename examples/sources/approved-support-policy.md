# Mapleline Dispatch Support Policy 2026

## Status

Approved by Support Operations. Effective April 1, 2026. This policy replaces the
2025 support policy for EU email tickets from that date.

## Service scope

This policy covers customer support requests submitted by email in the EU
queue. Phone tickets are governed by separate operating instructions.

## Response target

Send the first agent response within eight business hours. Business hours are
09:00–17:00 local time, Monday through Friday.
